## Inspirations

- [Final Fantasy Tactics](https://final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles.square-enix-games.com/): clear character selection and the satisfaction of committing a move.
- [A Highland Song](https://www.inklestudios.com/a-highland-song/): wonder, discovery and a meaningful journey.

- [Ravenhaul](https://bippinbits.itch.io/ravenhaul): a quick rhythm of planning, journeys and returns.
- [Tiny Glade](https://pouncelight.games/tiny-glade/): the pleasure of a small, living landscape.
- [Minami Lane](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2678990/Minami_Lane/): compact daily decisions and a warm sense of place.
- [Kanagawa](https://iellogames.com/games/kanagawa/): illustrated landscapes, seasonal colour and quiet choices.
- [Pokémon](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/business/games/): finding wildlife and filling a collection journal.
